## The New Zealand angle

The NZ registration date is the single most useful public fact here, and it is
worth being straight about why: it means Hexaware formally entered New Zealand in
early 2025. That is recent. A Kiwibank or TSB buyer will read that as *new
entrant* unless it is framed.

Two honest framings, pick one:

1. **The build-out.** Hexaware has been delivering into ANZ from Sydney for
   longer than it has had an NZ entity, and the entity is the commitment
   catching up to the work. Requires you to confirm the delivery history.
2. **The deliberate entrant.** Hexaware is building in New Zealand now, on
   purpose, and is looking for a small number of foundation clients rather than
   a long list. This is the more defensible one if the delivery history is thin,
   and it is a genuinely attractive proposition to a tier 2 bank that is tired of
   being a rounding error to a global SI.

Framing 2 is the recommendation until you tell me otherwise. It survives the
follow-up question; framing 1 does not unless there is real ANZ delivery behind
it.
