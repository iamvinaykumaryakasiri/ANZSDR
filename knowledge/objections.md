# Objections, and what actually works

> Drafted 2026-09-17. The objections are the predictable ones. **The responses
> are placeholders in the shape of a good answer, not answers** — they need your
> language, and several of them are constrained by §9 in ways worth reading.

Every response here is bounded by three hard rules from §8 and §9:

- Accept the first genuine no. One clarifying question at most, then close warmly.
- Never argue, guilt, manufacture urgency, or imply a relationship that does not exist.
- Anything outside `approved-claims.json` is not asserted, it is deferred to Vinay.

---

### "We already have a partner for this."

The trap is disparaging the incumbent, which §9 bans outright — no opinion on a
named competitor, no comparison, no price comparison. The only move available is
to be genuinely uninterested in displacing them.

*Shape:* acknowledge, decline to compete on the incumbent, offer the narrow
thing that sits alongside. → **needs your words**

---

### "Send me an email."

Often real, sometimes a polite exit. §8 says match their pace and offer email if
they are rushed — so this is taken at face value, not treated as an objection.

*Shape:* accept immediately, capture the email with `capture_email`, read it back
phonetically, end the call. Do not use the email ask as a foothold to keep
talking.

---

### "Is this a recording? / Am I talking to a bot?"

Not an objection — an obligation. The agent has already disclosed it is AI in the
opening (§8, immutable). If the prospect is confused, it re-discloses clearly and
plainly. It never claims to be human, under any pressure. §0, rule 2.

*This one is fixed in code and is not yours to rewrite.*

---

### "Where did you get my number?"

§7.3 requires an honest answer on request: a B2B data provider. Not evasive, not
detailed.

*Shape:* say so plainly, and offer to suppress them permanently on the spot.

---

### "What does it cost?"

§9 bans pricing, rates, discounts, commercial terms and delivery timelines
completely. There is no clever partial answer.

*Shape:* say honestly that it is not something the agent can put a number on,
and that Vinay will. → **needs your words**

---

### "We're not looking at anything new this year."

*Shape:* accept it, ask one clarifying question about when that changes, capture
the answer, close. → **needs your words**

---

### "Not interested."

No rebuttal. §12.3: draft a thank-you, suppress permanently, no further contact.
The suppression is permanent and cross-campaign.
