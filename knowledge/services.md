That matters for the claim index. A fact that comes from you rather than from a
public page is still a fact, but its source is *you*, and the index records it
that way (`source.kind: "operator"`). It is not weaker for that — it is arguably
stronger — but it should never be presented as something the prospect could look
up if they cannot.

Naming a colleague on a cold call also touches §9: *"anything about another
person at the account that wasn't publicly stated"* is banned, and while George
Mathew is a Hexaware person rather than a prospect's colleague, the same
instinct applies. Suggested rule for your confirmation: the agent may say the
DD&AI practice exists; it names George Mathew only if the prospect asks who runs
it.
