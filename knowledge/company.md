## The one-line version

Hexaware Technologies is a global IT services company — India-headquartered,
listed, Carlyle-backed — that does digital and AI-led transformation for large
enterprises. In ANZ the focus is BFSI, with New Zealand an active build-out.

## The figures, which do not agree

This is worth reading before approving anything numeric. Three public sources
give three different headcounts:

| Figure | Source | Date |
|---|---|---|
| 34,506 employees | SiliconANGLE, citing Hexaware's own Q2 results | as at 30 June 2026 |
| 31,884 employees | Revelio Labs | 2025 |
| "33,000+ employees, 68 offices" | secondary aggregators | undated |

And two revenue figures:

| Figure | Source |
|---|---|
| Q2 revenue US$405.4m, up 6.1% year on year | SiliconANGLE, citing Hexaware's quarterly results |
| FY2025 revenue ₹134.3bn (>US$1.4bn) | Craft.co |

**The recommendation is to approve only the two that trace to Hexaware's own
published results** — the 34,506 headcount and the Q2 revenue — and reject the
aggregator numbers. A cold call is the worst possible place to be caught with a
figure you cannot source. If you would rather the agent never quotes a number at
all, reject all five; the conversation design in §8 does not need one.

## What the agent should not do with this

Nothing here is a pitch. §8 targets 45–120 seconds and it is a
permission-to-continue call. Company scale is an answer to a question, not an
opening. If a prospect has not asked how big Hexaware is, the agent does not
tell them.
